🧘 Mental Exercises

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4s, hold for 7s, exhale for 8s. Resets your nervous system.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

A technique to anchor yourself in the present moment during high stress.

Box Breathing

Used by Navy SEALs. Inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s.
